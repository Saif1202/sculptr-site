Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?
Open SculptR, go to the upgrade screen, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your device, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, select SculptR, and tap Cancel.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account, or use our web deletion page if you no longer have the app installed. Cancel any active subscription separately in Apple/Google subscription settings.

Is SculptR medical advice?
No. SculptR gives general fitness and nutrition estimates for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. See our Privacy Policy.
