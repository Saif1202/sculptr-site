Delete your SculptR account

Two ways to permanently delete your account and data.

Option 1 · In the app (instant)

  1. Open SculptR and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 · By email (no app required)

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you within 7 days.

Email: support@sculptr-app.com

Subject: Delete my SculptR account

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted

Your profile, weigh-ins, food and workout logs, saved meals, plans, coach chat history, and account credentials are permanently removed from our systems.

What isn't deleted

Apple/Google subscription records are kept by Apple/Google for billing and tax purposes and are not affected by account deletion. Cancel your subscription separately in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (iOS) or Play Store → Payments and subscriptions (Android). Anonymised, aggregate analytics that can't be tied back to you may be retained.

See our full Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used.
